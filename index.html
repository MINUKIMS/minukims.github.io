I am a doctoral researcher in the Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory (SAIL) at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Shrikanth (Shri) Narayanan.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of computational linguistics and speech artificial intelligence. In particular, I explore phonetics, phonology, and language typology, connecting linguistic theories with scalable representation learning. My research focuses on developing inclusive speech technologies, including low-resource language transfer, non-native/atypical speech modeling, and phonetic representations.
