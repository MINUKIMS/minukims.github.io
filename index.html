Welcome to my homepage!
I am a Ph.D. student and Doctoral Researcher in the Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory (SAIL) at the University of Southern California (USC).
Advised by Prof. Shrikanth (Shri) Narayanan
NAE Member · Guggenheim Fellow · Fellow of IEEE / ISCA / ACM / AAAS / ASA / APS / AIMBE / NAI / AAAC


My academic interests lie at the intersection of linguistics and speech artificial intelligence. In particular, I explore phonetics, phonology, and language typology, connecting linguistic theory with scalable machine learning representations.

My research focuses on advancing inclusive speech technologies (e.g., low-resource language transfer, atypical/dysarthric speech, and phonetic representation learning).
