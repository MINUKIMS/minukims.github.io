Welcome to my homepage!
I am a doctoral researcher in the Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory (SAIL) at the University of Southern California (USC), advised by Prof. Shrikanth (Shri) Narayanan (NAE Member, Guggenheim Fellow, Fellow of IEEE/ISCA/ACM/AAAS/ASA/APS/AIMBE/NAI/AAAC).

My academic interests lie at the intersection of linguistics and artificial intelligence. In particular, I enjoy exploring phonetics, phonology, and language typology, connecting linguistic theories with computational and representation learning approaches.

My research focuses on applying this interdisciplinary perspective to inclusive speech technologies (e.g., low-resource language transfer, non-native/atypical speech, and phonetic representation learning).
